1. Parties

Who this contract is between

This agreement is between:

  1. Red Face, the technology party, which holds 3%.
  2. The business partner, who holds 3%. The full legal name is written on the signature page before signing.
  3. Darren, the commercial and investment party. His surname is written on the signature page. He holds the balance of the shares.

Print two copies. All three parties initial every page of both copies, and all three sign both copies. Each party keeps one fully signed copy. A party has not agreed until that party has initialled every page and signed the last page.

2. Purpose

What the parties are building

The parties agree to build and commercialise PAY, a smart payment terminal and value-added-services platform. The aim is a small business in a box: one device through which a person can take payments and sell airtime, data, electricity, and other approved digital products.

Red Face has already built the software foundation and has shown it. Darren’s side has said they know the acquiring bank, the PSP, and the people who supply card machines. This contract records that split of work, and the shares that go with it.

3. Shares

Shareholding

The parties agree the shareholding as follows. The technology side is 6% in total. That 6% is split into two equal parts of 3%. The balance is held by Darren.

HolderShareWhat that share is for
Red Face3%The software, the product, and the system already built and shown.
Business partner3%Participation on the technology side, beside Red Face. Full legal name on the signature page.
Darren94%The balance. Commercial and investment side, including the bank, the PSP, the machines, and the VAS commercial relationship.
Total100%3% + 3% + 94%.
No other percentage was agreed. If any part of the 3% business-partner share is to be split with another person, that split is written on the signature page before anyone signs. If it is not written there, it is not part of this contract.

These percentages are the agreed shareholding for the project company. They are not a salary. Pay for ongoing work, if any, is written in a separate compensation note. Signing this agreement does not by itself issue share certificates. When the company is incorporated, the shares are issued in these percentages. Until then, this signed agreement is the record of who holds what.

What Red Face has already put on the table

Darren acknowledges that Red Face has built and shown the following. This is the technology contribution already made, and it is the reason for the Red Face 3%.

5. Darren’s obligations

What Darren must bring

Red Face has brought the software. Darren has said his side knows the acquiring bank, the PSP, and the people around the card machines. He must bring those relationships, and he must also bring the VAS commercial side. The software connection to a provider is not a substitute for that commercial relationship.

Darren agrees to introduce, open, and carry through the following. Each item is a deliverable of this contract, not a favour.

DeliverableWhat “brought” means
Acquiring bankA named acquiring bank, a working introduction, and a path for PAY to be accepted as a merchant-acquiring product. The name of the bank is written on the signature page.
PSPA named payment service provider who will process live card transactions for the terminals, with a sandbox or pilot account the software can connect to.
Certified card machinesThe supplier of the certified payment hardware, the device model, and the first machines for a controlled pilot. PAY will not collect card numbers or PINs. The machine talks to the acquirer.
VAS commercial sideThe commercial relationship for airtime, data, electricity, and other approved products: who the wholesale or network party is, the buying price, the allowed selling price, and how settlement works. Red Face has already connected the software. Darren brings the commercial supply.
Pilot accessThe contacts, accounts, and documents the bank, the PSP, the device supplier, and the VAS party require, so a controlled pilot can start.
If Darren does not bring the acquiring bank, the PSP, the certified machines, and the VAS commercial side, the commercial half of this project cannot start. That obligation sits with Darren. The software half has already been shown.

6. Red Face’s continuing work

What Red Face still does

Red Face continues the engineering of PAY: the terminal application, the cloud platform, device management, security, and the connections to the bank, PSP, machines, and VAS supply once Darren has brought them. Red Face does not source the acquiring bank, the PSP contract, the certified hardware supply, or the VAS wholesale relationship. Those are Darren’s deliverables under clause 5.

The business partner participates on the technology side with Red Face. The business partner does not replace Darren’s obligation to bring the bank, the PSP, the machines, or the VAS commercial side.

7. Company and property

The project company

The parties will use a company for PAY, or form one. Shares in that company are issued in the percentages in clause 3. The software built for PAY is for the project. When the company exists, that software is held for the company. Red Face will not withhold the prototype from the project after this agreement is signed.

A later shareholders’ agreement may add vesting, leaver terms, and director rules. It may not change the 3%, 3%, and 94% unless all three parties sign that change.

8. Next work, in order

What happens after signature

  1. Darren writes the bank name, the PSP name, the machine supplier, and the VAS commercial party on the signature page, or within 14 days after signature.
  2. Darren opens the introductions and the pilot accounts.
  3. Red Face connects PAY to the real PSP and the certified machine. Card and tap leave the practice flow only when that connection exists.
  4. The parties set the VAS markup and the merchant commission in writing. Until then, no margin is promised.
  5. The parties run a controlled pilot and measure whether a merchant actually earns.
